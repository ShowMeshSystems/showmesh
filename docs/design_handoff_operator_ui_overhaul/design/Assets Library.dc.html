What refers to it

Nothing refers to an asset by id. Cues and playlists name the sequence, and the coordinator resolves whichever version is current at the moment it looks — so replacing bytes here changes what tonight plays, with nothing to re-point.

In this show, carol-of-the-bells is named by 2 cues and 1 playlist.
